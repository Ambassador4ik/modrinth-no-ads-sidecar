# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Harassment, threats, discriminatory
language, doxxing, impersonation, and deliberate disruption are not accepted.
Technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks are not.

Do not use this project to request or share credentials, session material,
private advertising data, malware, account-entitlement spoofing, authentication
bypasses, updater-signature bypasses, or instructions for testing systems
without authorization.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, release
comments, and private reports sent to the maintainer about this project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[thebluedawn@outlook.com](mailto:thebluedawn@outlook.com). Include links or
screenshots and enough context to assess the report. The maintainer may edit,
hide, lock, or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to protect contributors and the project.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Retaliation against someone
who makes a good-faith report is prohibited.
